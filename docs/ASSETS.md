# Asset notes

## Artwork

The three included environment images are original AI-generated illustrations created for this portfolio template:

- `orbital-*`: a dark cinematic orbital outpost and mountain canyon.
- `dune-*`: monumental desert ruins in warm amber light.
- `grove-*`: a luminous forest and ancient architectural gate.

They are illustrative placeholders, not screenshots of actual completed games. Replace them with authentic project media when presenting your work. Full-size files are 1672 × 941 pixels, encoded as lossless WebP. The 768px variants are resized derivatives, also encoded losslessly.

## Video

The example YouTube URL is `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ` (Big Buck Bunny). It is an external demonstration player, clearly labeled in the page, and is not downloaded or represented as gameplay from a sample project. Video availability and embedding permissions are controlled by YouTube and the uploader.

## Icons and fonts

The included small SVG interface icons are inline path artwork; no external icon package is required. System font stacks are used. Add only fonts and media you have permission to distribute to the matching asset folders.

## Identity and links

The name, biography, phone, email, résumé, project claims, and links are sample content. External social links currently lead to the service homepages. Replace them with your actual public profile and project URLs before publishing.
